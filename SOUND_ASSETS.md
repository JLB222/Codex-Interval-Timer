# Sound assets

The three WAV effects in `assets/` were created specifically for this project with procedural sine-wave synthesis:

- `active-to-rest.wav`: two descending notes for the rest interval.
- `rest-to-active.wav`: two ascending notes for the next active interval.
- `workout-complete.wav`: a short layered celebratory chime.

These original recordings are dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). No third-party recording is included.
